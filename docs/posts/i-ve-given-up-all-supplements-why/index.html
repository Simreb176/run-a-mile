I’ve given up all supplements. Why?

25 October 2026 · 2 min read

I’ve always believed you should take some supplements, at least that was the case until quite recently.

When I turned 50, I read I should be on a 50+ multi vitamin. So I took heed and took one religiously, then over the years I took amongst others regularly or as needed:

  • high dose vitamin D3,
  • Cod liver oil,
  • Vitamin C,
  • Ferritin tablets,
  • calcium, magnesium & zinc combinations,
  • ginseng & ginkgo biloba,
  • Glucosamine & Chondroitin,
  • Creatine

All these were sold to me through the health and wellness press through adverts, info-commercials, industry experts or recommendations from friends.

Then about a year ago something changed and I started reading about the interaction of these supplements stopping each other from working and possibly some prescription medicines too.

Some reports claimed if you were on a healthy diet, you didn’t need these. Surely this can’t be true I had been led to believe. I wasn’t sure so continued my regime.

Then the reports started about the bulking agents not being any good for you and you should be taking purer forms of supplements and excessive amounts of some supplements affecting health in some meaningful ways and you should stop.

Then in early August 2026 I read an Apple News/Sunday Times article “Why you should bin all your Supplements” by the tv Doctor, Dr. Xand Van Tulleken. What an eye opener.

The article bases was all your supplements were a waste of money, they aren’t working and the industry on the whole hasn’t got any real proof they do.

I bought his book “Make me well” and it has changed my life and I indeed binned all my supplements after reading the first 3 chapters.

My wife also read the “Make me Well” book and stopped taking Collagen immediately after reading about it and she also highly recommends that you read the chapter on Big Menopause.

I give Platelets at the blood donor clinic every 2-3 weeks and had been taking iron supplements in the belief I needed them.

I have given twice since I stopped taking all my supplements and remarkably my blood iron has naturally increased, I feel much better all round and on top of it all I am saving money every month.

I recommend you read the book if you can borrow a copy, already a best seller and also look at the NHS (UK) website as it has the most sensible approach to supplements, and advises on safe levels if you are in need. See your GP to find out if you have a deficit in any of these vitamins and minerals and take if GP recommends.
