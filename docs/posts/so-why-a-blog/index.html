So why a blog?

23 October 2026 · 2 min read

Blogs are so Personal and I would like to share and record my journey back to running or jogging, depending on your point of view.

I have been diagnosed with moderate to severe osteoarthritis in my right hip and been told I need a full hip replacement.

“I’m not ready for that”

I have always been reasonably active and enjoyed playing many different sports over my life. I like walking and see myself as a jogger rather than a runner.

This blog is mainly to record how I get back to running just 1 mile, or jogging. This may seem a lot to some but I have missed it over the last six months.

I have recently read reports saying that if you have been a runner/jogger most of your life this shouldn’t impose too much strain on your osteoarthritis. I will go into this in more detail in a separate blog.

I’ve also read that slow running, a Japanese idea puts less strain on your joints. Again, I will go into more detail in another blog. I will try and put slow running into action and will report back.

I have read, been given advice by both medical and non-medical people and I will try each and every recomendation to see what merits improvement for me and write a balanced blog for each one. Always open to suggestions too.

Before I start to run I need to get some weight off. I have had some success already and lost 7lb. Not much I know and need to lose another 12 lbs to get back to the weight I was before I was diagnosed with hip OsteoArthritus. Again I will blog regularly about this and whatever is on my mind regarding weight loss at the time.

My next blog will be on supplements and what I have found best to do recently, and the inspiration behind it. May be an eye opener to some, it was to me. I have a great example to share.

That’s it for now.
