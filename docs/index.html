A running journey

To “Run a Mile” - to avoid something

Can you return to running with Hip OsteoArthritis?

When you mention running to some, they would literally “run a mile” not to run.

This website is dedicated to my attempt to return to running, but only to “run a mile” literally.

For most of my life, I’ve been reasonably active, jogging, tennis, cricket, sailing, swimming & cycling. I am no athlete but I want to get back to be able to "run a mile" again.

A couple of years ago I stated to slow down running. I was never that quick to start with, with around a 30 minute 5k but I saw this sliding quite quickly to around 38 minutes and just thought “getting older”.

Had a couple of injuries and rather than a week to return, it took longer, sometimes a month for me to get back to running.

Then on return from a winter break in March 2025, I couldn’t get up and had a searing pain and my hip had locked. Over a 19 day period this progressed in pain so I saw my GP, had an x-ray and saw a Physio to get me back to running after a diagnosis of moderate osteoarthritis. 3 months later I started back on a special running scheme and went to the gym 3 times a week, yoga and Pilates and ran a parkrun in 44 mins 2 more months later.

From here I then blew a knee bursa on the same leg and had some more time away from general exercise with a new physio approach and this cycle continued until I was told I needed a new hip and told to stop running.

I decided I didn’t need a hip replacement yet as the pain had subsided as I had got stronger in the gym.

Weight was another problem, putting on 1 1/2 stone during this time. Always on the slightly well built side, this extra weight needs to be shifted to return to running to put less stress on the joints.

I will develop a section on how I will lose the weight whilst protecting or improving muscle density.

So come on a journey with me, with early morning starts, heavy legs and the small wins — documented one honest walk or run at a time. Welcome to running a mile, in the sense nobody probably ever intended.
